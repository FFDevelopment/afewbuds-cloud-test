# Bongchester districts

Desktop 0.13.1 and mobile 3D v12 share the same world-space district lookup.

| District | Current area |
| --- | --- |
| Roachwood | Starter apartment, Central Market and first house; west of x=73 |
| Half Baked Heights | Eastern homes and park; x=73 to the police cross street at x=139 |
| Paranoia Point | Police district; x=139 eastward, including the station's upper floor |

The mobile top HUD displays the city and district persistently on two lines over its dark panel. Desktop keeps the location/time/cash detail below the city/district. Interior close-ups retain the player's district. No district-change toast is added.

Reserved for future map development: Cottonmouth Commons (shopping), Bongwater Bay (waterfront/industry), Couchlock Court (cul-de-sac), Joint Junction (downtown), High Society Hills (wealthy homes), Munchie Mile (food/night stores), The Sticks (rural properties). These areas are not built yet; adding a name does not imply a new playable region.
