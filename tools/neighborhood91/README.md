# .91 door manager and contact action toggles

Assigned apartment dealers switch off automatic street sales until returned using the same contact action, which reads Go Back to Street Deals while assigned. Generic and named dealers remain in the crew roster for assignments and accounting. Door arrivals wait for the manager to reach the door; existing waiting visits can also be served, whether the player is home or away. Normal clients use real requested quantities and strains, without the street pool's prior personal-sale restriction. Special encounters remain manual. Failed orders notify the player; paused play cannot consume an attempt or sell.

Door orders combine Dealer Locker stock, unreserved apartment product storage and ready packaged bench inventory, in that order. Insufficient stock cannot cause partial consumption or revenue. Raw/unpacked product and reserved grams remain protected. Original dealer commission, nightly settlement, revenue, heat and advancement accounting is reused. Released visits cannot sell twice; later legitimate door visits can buy again.

Contact actions stay on their current screen and toggle: Close Shop / Open Shop, Shut Down Shop & Lay Low / Set Up Shop, Handle Apartment Door / Go Back to Street Deals. Regular Close leaves production and power unchanged; Lay Low retains the full lights/blinds/work shutdown. Redundant apartment assignment and unavailable house buttons removed from the crew action list.

Validation: actual visitor service at home and away, combined sources, reservations, shortages, no duplicate sales, street exclusion, pause/resume, inverse contact actions, original accounting, and verified browser pack loader. Includes .90 mobile HUD and fertilizer pickup ordering.
