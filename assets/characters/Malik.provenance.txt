DESIGN
  Smooth-shaded, simplified Malik with braids, beard, black collarless FFD shirt,
  dark jeans, original sneakers and original forearm tattoo motifs.
  No third-party clothing marks or purchased/generated meshes are included.
  This is a first likeness and deformation study, not a final likeness match.
  The tattoos are original simplified motifs, not traced reference tattoos.

RUNTIME ASSET
  godot/assets/Malik.glb             Self-contained skinned model and 3 test clips
  godot/assets/Malik_BaseColor.png   Editable 2048 x 2048 color atlas
  godot/assets/Malik.tscn            Godot wrapper using the external color atlas
  godot/assets/malik_material.gd     Wrapper material setup

  23,821 triangles; 24,386 exported vertices including UV seams; 1 material.
  56 bones; normalized skin weights, maximum 4 influences per vertex.
  Metres, +Y up, -Z forward. The master bone names, order, parents, and
  identity rest bases are preserved. Rest pose is a T-pose.
  Blender has different display-bone axes. Use the included custom exporter
  when rebuilding; a default Blender GLB export changes the rest-basis contract.

REVIEW CONTROLS
  Open godot/project.godot in Godot 4.7.2 and press F6 on review.tscn or F5.
  1 = idle, 2 = walk, 3 = bend test, 4 = T-pose.
  Drag left mouse to orbit, wheel to zoom, Space to pause/resume.
  The clips are preview motions, not finished gameplay locomotion or actions.

EDITING TEXTURES LATER
  In the Malik.tscn wrapper, the external PNG overrides the embedded GLB atlas.
  Edit the PNG without changing its layout; Godot reimports the color changes.
  For larger revisions use Texture Paint on the atlas UVs in Blender, or edit
  the named source materials and rebuild. Skin, clothes, hair and embroidery
  retain separate authoring material slots in the Blender source.
  The standalone GLB contains its original embedded atlas; use the wrapper to
  see external PNG changes without repacking that file.

INTEGRATION
  Copy the assets into your character folder, then adjust the wrapper's three
  resource paths to match the new location. Alternatively import Malik.glb alone.
  Put the visual scene under an NPC CharacterBody3D; keep navigation/collision
  on that gameplay parent. Select idle on its AnimationPlayer for an arms-down
  pose. The visual origin is at ground level.
  This model already includes the matching skeleton. Do not parent it under a
  second master skeleton and assume its skin bindings transfer automatically.
  Bone attachment children are provided for hands, headwear, back and mouth;
  prop grips still require calibration against the final animations.

VERIFIED
  source/static_validation.json: GLB structure, exact ordered bone contract,
  inverse binds, unit normals, skin weights, indices, atlas and triangle budget.
  source/godot_validation.json: imported Skeleton3D, all rest transforms,
  bound mesh, normalized weights and finite poses for all 3 imported clips.
  Godot 4.7.2 OpenGL Compatibility render on the local RTX 3070 was inspected.
  A separate local AFewBuds room copy was used for appearance/scale review.
  The original game project and original save directory were not edited.

REMAINING BEFORE PRODUCTION
  Further face/hand likeness and cloth refinement, finished movement/action
  animation, collision/navigation integration, phone profiling and LODs.
  No facial blend shapes, lip sync, IK controls or smoke effects are included.
  The current polygon count is a first close-view asset, not a proven mobile
  crowd budget. No claim of target-device performance is made.

SOURCE / PROVENANCE
  Geometry, atlas, code and sample motions were authored locally for AFewBuds
  from the user's Malik reference and AFB_MasterRig_v1.zip. The reference image
  is not redistributed. No paid generation services or third-party models were
  used. The game-dev CLI was not available; checks are the included local Python
  and Godot checks, not game-dev canonical-package certification.
  source/build_malik.py reproduces the mesh, atlas, GLB, Blender scene and renders.
  source/master_rig/ preserves the supplied master rig and its documentation.
