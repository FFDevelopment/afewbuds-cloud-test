# Equipment items preview

Paired experiment for cloud test and desktop 3D prototype. Existing starter tents, plants, equipment tiers and furniture become owned items; no repurchase is required.

- Open Central Market > Upgrades for grow tents and equipment, or Furniture for household items. Tents hold 1, 2, 3 or 4 plants. Extra tents must be bought and placed; slot upgrades no longer spawn tents.
- Choose Backpack or an owned property's curb as the delivery destination. Boxes are ready immediately in this prototype. Stand beside a delivery to transfer its items into the backpack.
- Phone > Real Estate > Arrange Furniture, or computer equipment management: unlock an empty item, then Move or Pick up. In Backpack select the packed item and Place. Walk and aim normally, rotate, and confirm when green.
- Plants (including mature/dead plants), stock, untrimmed buds, trimmed buds, packaged product, and active work block pickup, moving and upgrades. Empty the item or finish/cancel the work first. Reserved orders must be completed/cancelled before changing the active operation.
- Equipment identity, upgrades and tent slots persist through pickup, storage, placement and reload. Backpack weight still applies. Sell carried equipment at Central Market for 50% of its paid value, including upgrades.
- Upgrading an empty owned station costs the difference between tiers. Better equipment can also be bought outright. The starter tent can receive a light-kit improvement without spawning another tent.
- Relocation changes the active operation but leaves equipment and stock at their physical property. Carry supplies and empty equipment to the new property. Chapter progress is retained.
- Test progress uses the existing isolated preview saves. Production/main and live career uploads are unchanged.

Cloud: https://ffdevelopment.github.io/afewbuds-cloud-test/expansion-preview/
Desktop Windows: https://github.com/FFDevelopment/afewbuds-3d-prototype/releases/tag/v0.16.0-equipment.1

Large deliveries currently appear as boxes immediately; a delivery vehicle/timed courier sequence is not implemented. New catalog items use functional prototype models.
