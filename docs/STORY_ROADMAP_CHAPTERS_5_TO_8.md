# AFewBuds Story Roadmap - Chapters 5 to 8

## Chapter 5 - Building an Operation

Purpose: turn the first house into a real money sink and make relocation feel like a new phase, not a bigger apartment.

Milestones:
- Make It Yours: place and lock two house furniture pieces.
- Put Money Into the Walls: install at least $2,500 of house furniture or equipment.
- Backroom Reserve: install at least 400g of house storage capacity.
- Room to Grow: place a house grow-room tent and harvest three plants.
- A Consistent Batch: trim 30g and seal five bags.
- House Regulars: complete five personal sales and two dealer sales from the house operation.
- Crew Has a Place: cover three staff roles and complete 60 production-worker tasks.
- Keep the Lights On: pay a house utility bill and clear overdue property balances.
- Open House, Quiet Business: reach $3,500 new revenue, twelve personal sales, Heat at or below 25, then meet Rod.

Spend targets:
- House furniture and equipment.
- Larger storage and dealer cabinets.
- Better grow canopy and supply cabinet.
- Higher-tier packing/sealing station.
- Bills and property balances.

## Chapter 6 - Joint Junction

Purpose: make the player expand beyond a single residential operation into a small public-facing brand.

Milestones:
- Scout the Spot: Rod introduces a small storefront opportunity.
- Paper Front: choose a legal-looking business cover.
- Build the Counter: buy counter, display, register, storage and security fixtures.
- Soft Open: serve limited walk-in customers while keeping illegal inventory hidden.
- Supplier Relationship: spend on a reliable supply contact or delivery lane.
- Staff the Front: hire or assign a front-counter worker.
- Keep It Clean: pass several low-Heat days while operating.
- Junction Launch: hold the first real launch day.

Spend targets:
- Storefront lease or down payment.
- Counter/display fixtures.
- Security camera system.
- Front-room decor and signage.
- Inventory backroom upgrades.
- Staff wages and training.

## Chapter 7 - Distribution Pressure

Purpose: turn success into operational pressure: more money, more heat, and more logistics.

Milestones:
- Dealer Routes: unlock multiple delivery zones.
- Route Lockers: install zone-specific stock lockers.
- Driver Problem: buy or upgrade a delivery vehicle.
- Bad Batch Recovery: handle a failed or delayed batch without losing customers.
- Pressure Week: survive a high-demand week.
- Quiet Route: run a low-profile delivery stretch with reduced Heat.
- Trusted Network: raise several regulars into route-level clients.
- City Coverage: prove the operation can move volume outside the home/storefront.

Spend targets:
- Vehicles.
- Route lockers.
- Driver/dealer wages.
- Emergency replacement stock.
- Heat-management services.
- Better packaging and labeling.

## Chapter 8 - Citywide Brand

Purpose: make late-game money matter by pushing the player toward brand identity, premium clients and risk choices.

Milestones:
- Brand Identity: choose a brand direction.
- Premium Menu: maintain several high-grade products.
- VIP Clients: unlock and retain high-demand clients.
- Lab Upgrade: invest in genetics or strain consistency.
- Event Drop: prepare a limited drop.
- Clean Books: keep property, utility and staff balances current.
- Reputation Run: reach a high brand/reputation tier.
- Citywide Name: complete the first citywide brand finale.

Spend targets:
- Branding/signage.
- Premium packaging.
- Genetics lab upgrades.
- VIP lounge/showroom pieces.
- High-capacity storage.
- Staff and route scaling.

## Furniture Direction

- Market furniture is a starter/catalog layer, not final art.
- Final pieces need AFewBuds style tags, fit-safe dimensions, interaction type and NPC use points.
- Usable furniture should define approach clearances and animation intent before it becomes story-critical.
- Existing apartment furniture remains the visual benchmark until a replacement is proven in-game.
